Module 9 Study Check

Review the questions below and compare your answers with the key provided.

1. According to Romans 13:1-7, why should believers submit to governing authorities?

A. Because authorities are always perfect
B. Because authority exists only through God and is ordained by Him
C. Because submission guarantees wealth and success
D. Because believers have no choice in any circumstance

Answer: B. Because authority exists only through God and is ordained by Him

2. What was the primary sin that led to Satan’s fall according to Isaiah 14 and Ezekiel 28?

A. Ignorance of God’s commands
B. Lack of worship
C. Rebellion against God’s authority and desire for self-exaltation
D. Failure to serve the angels

Answer: C. Rebellion against God’s authority and desire for self-exaltation

3. According to the lesson, what is the greatest demand found throughout Scripture?

A. Sacrifice and religious activity
B. Spiritual gifts and miracles
C. Obedience to God’s will and authority
D. Financial generosity

Answer: C. Obedience to God’s will and authority

4. What lesson does Paul’s conversion on the Damascus Road teach about authority?

A. Education is the key to spiritual growth.
B. God only uses highly respected leaders.
C. True submission to God includes submission to His delegated authority.
D. Personal independence is necessary for ministry.

Answer: C. True submission to God includes submission to His delegated authority.

5. What mistake did King Saul make in 1 Samuel 15?

A. He refused to fight the Amalekites.
B. He obeyed God completely.
C. He replaced obedience with his own idea of sacrifice.
D. He failed to offer any sacrifice at all.

Answer: C. He replaced obedience with his own idea of sacrifice.

6. According to the notes, which of the following is NOT a characteristic of rebellion?

A. Self-will
B. Independence from God’s authority
C. Humble teachability
D. Disobedience

Answer: C. Humble teachability

7. What was the primary error of Nadab and Abihu when they offered “strange fire” before the Lord?

A. They used the wrong altar.
B. They acted independently without submission to God’s established authority.
C. They forgot the prescribed sacrifices.
D. They were not members of Aaron’s family.

Answer: B. They acted independently without submission to God’s established authority.

Bonus Critical Thinking Question

How do the examples of Satan, Saul, Nadab and Abihu, and Paul demonstrate the difference between serving God through obedience and serving through self-will?

A. They show that results matter more than motives.
B. They demonstrate that God values obedience to His authority above personal ambition, sacrifice, or religious activity.
C. They prove that leadership positions guarantee God’s approval.
D. They teach that independence is necessary for spiritual maturity.

Answer: B. They demonstrate that God values obedience to His authority above personal ambition, sacrifice, or religious activity.
