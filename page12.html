Module 10 Study Check

Answer the questions below and compare with the key provided.

1. What is cessationism?

a. The belief that the gifts of the Spirit and the power manifestations of the Spirit ceased when the apostles died
b. God does not speak to His people anymore, He just left the Bible
c. There is no more prophecy
d. We can cease from works of faith and obedience to the Law

Answer: a

2. Why is there such an interest in the occult and witchcraft in the younger generation now?

a. They are not seeing the power of God in the Church
b. They want control over things
c. They do not think it is evil or Satanic in origin
d. A, and c

Answer: d

3. Why does the idea of cessationism continue?

a. Ignorance
b. Misunderstanding of the purpose of the Holy Spirit to the Church
c. No one is challenging it
d. A and B

Answer: d

4. What is the purpose of the Holy Spirit to the Church?

a. To equip believers with power and demonstration of the glory of God in order to minister to others and spread the gospel
b. To reveal Jesus to us in the Word
c. To guide and direct, correct, warn, lead and encourage believers.
d. A, b, and c

Answer: d

5. What did Jesus say blaspheming the Holy Spirit meant?

a. Saying the name of the Lord in vain
b. Calling God a liar
c. Saying a work of God was a work of the devil
d. Cursing the name of Jesus or what He did

Answer: c

6. What signs did Jesus say would follow any who believed in Him in His Great Commission in Mark 16?

a. Just to go preach the gospel everywhere
b. Handle snakes in church to prove you have no fear and they have no power over you
c. cast out demons, speak with new tongues, take up serpents, lay hands on the sick, if they drink anything deadly it won’t harm them
d. they would be prophets who spoke to the nations

Answer: c

7. Can the gifts of the Spirit be earned?

a. Yes
b. No

Answer: b

8. What is the purpose of the gifts of the Holy Spirit?

a. To make one look more Godly and spiritual
b. To further one’s ministry
c. To love people with the love of God
d. To prove God is real

Answer: c

9. What is the more excellent way?

a. Faith
b. Love
c. Truth
d. Hope

Answer: b

10. What does exhort mean?

a. motivating others in God, encourage, to urge strongly, to admonish, to entreat, to call upon personally and plead with someone to take a specific course of action.
b. To correct, rebuke
c. To argue a spiritual point

Answer: a

11. What is one of the purposes of tongues?

a. To minister to others
b. To build up our own spirit
c. To look spiritual
d. To tell the devil what to do in God’s language

Answer: a

12. What did the Lord say are the gifts of the five-fold ministry?

a. Apostle, prophet, evangelist, teacher, pastor
b. Giving, serving, fasting, prayer, deliverance
c. Preaching, casting out demons, laying hands on the sick, taking up serpents, raising the dead
d. Teacher, pastor, prophet, bishop, evangelist

Answer: a

13. What makes one an apostle?

a. They help start churches
b. They help counsel and mentor other pastors
c. Self-proclaimed on a business card because they pastor a church
d. Someone referred to them as such
e. A and B

Answer: e
